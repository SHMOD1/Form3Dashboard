Say vs. See

Comparing what residents reported in community interviews (Form 3) against what field auditors physically measured (Form 1 & 2), per drain. Drains are ranked by the size of the gap between the two, in either direction — residents reporting more distress than the physical audit shows, or a physically severe drain that residents aren't flagging as a problem. Both kinds of gaps are worth investigating further.
